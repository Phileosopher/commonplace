
# How to defend yourself

Personal defense is a series of factors:

1. It begins with [situational awareness](safety-streetsmart.md), which avoids most potential risks.
2. You should have readily-available skills beforehand (e.g., martial arts training, gun training).
3. You should have some readily-available weapons beforehand (e.g., pepper spray, a gun).
4. At the moment of an engagement, your prepared skills, tools, and awareness determine what your [creativity](mind-creativity-how.md) can use at the moment.

## At your home

Turn off the gas and electricity outside the house, which can create fire hazards.

- You also have a familiarity advantage by fighting in the dark.
- You can also use neck wires and low wire entanglement in the dark to slow them and give you time to get out of a lost room.

Outside the house, two rolls of wire secured by pickets and liberally sown with anti-personnel mines will deter the enemy.

Cut a hole for observation in the attic, in the wall, or underneath the eaves.

Use curtains or sacking to cover the windows, and use wire neeting to keep out grenades or satchel charges.

Add two layers of sandbags on the bottom floor to stop the enemy from shooting through it.

To prevent a window intrusion, remove floorboards below downstairs windows and place nails or broken glass between the floor spars.

Cut small holes in the floors so that you can drop explosives from above them.

If you have a chimney, fill it with barbed wire.

Use holes cut in the floors with ladders or knotted rope as temporary stairways.

Remove the plaster from the ceiling to avoid it falling later, which can be useful for sandbags or filler elsewhere.

When all other preparation is complete, remove the stairs or comver them with boards with protruding nails.

## Specific location

At the gas pump

- If you pull back the front part of the nozzle, you can spray gasoline on people.
- This is a little finicky, so practice beforehand at least a little.
