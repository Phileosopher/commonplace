
# Exodus commentary

## 12:1-30

This story is God's direct vengeance against Egypt's complicit actions (especially Pharaoh's) in destroying the Hebrews in 1:22.

- However, God is not delivering Talionic justice (eye for an eye), since it is open to everyone willing to accept the blood sacrifice.
- In other words, a payment absolutely has to be made, but that payment may come from an intermediary, and this directly points in a symbolism to Christ.

## 34

The Ten Commandments written on the tablets are uniquely different than the first Ten Commandments in Exodus 20.

- There are multiple theories as to why.
  - One theory is that they're samples of the Law indicated throughout Leviticus.
  - Another theory is that it's the distinction in Hebrews 7 between the order of Aaron vs. the order of Melchizedek.
