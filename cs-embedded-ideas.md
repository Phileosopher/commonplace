
# Ideas for embedded devices

To start with, you could easily make a self-sufficient, fully-functioning [personal computer](cs-hardware.md).

- It will often have to be [Linux](cs-os-unix.md), but you can also make it a Windows thin client to remotely access a separate, more powerful [Windows](cs-os-windows.md) computer.
- While it's a bit clunky, you can even make a smartphone with the right parts (e.g., [OURS project](https://github.com/evanman83/OURS-project/)).
