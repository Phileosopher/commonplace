
# Logical proofs of God's existence

Consciousness

1. Mental states are real, and are nonphysical.
2. There isn't, and cannot be, a scientific explanation for mental states.
3. This consciousness exists for some type of clear connection with God.

Liebniz

1. Everything has an explanation for it.
2. Therefore, God explains the universe's existence.

Fine-Tuning/Teleological

1. The universe is highly ordered and necessary for [life](science-life.md) to exist.
2. Something must have fine-tuned it.
3. It's absurd to presume it came from pure randomness.
4. God was the fine-tuner.

Moral/Axiological

1. There is an objective [evil](morality-evil.md).
2. To have an objective evil, there must be an objective [morality](morality.md).
3. Morality requires God to exist.
4. Morals do exist.
5. God exists

- This argument is very old (first proposed by Socrates in his "Euthyphro").
- It also happens to also nullify the [Evil God Challenge](philosophy-paradoxes.md).

Ontological argument

1. There's something that's the "greatest" to compare everything to.
2. That comparison is God.

Proof of the Truthful/First Cause/Cosmological/Kalam

1. Some things only exist because other things exist ("contingent" things).
2. While almost everything is contingent, not *everything* can be contingent because it would loop back on itself.
3. Therefore, there is a necessary non-contingent thing that must exist.
4. That necessary thing is God.

- This argument was first made by the Muslim philosopher Avicenna.

Absolute Logic/Transcendent

1. The laws of [logic](logic.md) exist as something that pre-existed humans.
2. All conceptual laws reflect the mind of a law-giver.
3. The most reasonable conclusion for that law-giver is God.

Uniqueness/Anthropic

1. Our universe is uniquely built for life to exist and for that life to examine the universe.
2. That unique design isn't the product of random chance or unguided probabilities.
3. There is a designer for the universe we can call God.

## Specific conditions

Earth's placement:

- The earth must be close enough to have the materials necessary for life, but it can't be any closer or it'd kill everything with radiation.
- It has to have had the right tilt to give seasons and a sustainable [ecology](science-ecology.md), but not so much that it would kill everything from the polar extremes.

The moon is necessary to stabilize the earth's axial tilt.

- The moon is also necessary to create tides to send oxygen deep into the ocean.

The earth's properties:

- It needs to be the right mixture of air in the atmosphere.
- It must have the right atmospheric thickness.
- It must have the right mass to hold the right atmosphere.
- It needs the right geology.
  - It must have molten magma rotating under the earth to create a magnetic shield that blankets against cosmic radiation.

The solar system blocks celestial objects from hitting the earth.

Life is enormously complex:

- All cells are absurdly complex.
  - For a flagella to exist, it requires 30 components, and can rotate at around 100K RPM and can change direction in a quarter-turn.
  - Every cell has highly advanced microscopic delivery mechanisms, advanced DNA copying systems, energy generation structures, energy movement structures, and duplication systems.
- In all this complexity, molecules have never, ever drifted toward forming life.
