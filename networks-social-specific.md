
# Specific social network trivia

## Fediverse

Here are at least some of the fediverse services/platforms available.

- Most of them use the ActivityPub protocol, unless otherwise specified.
- [GNU social](https://gnusocial.rocks/) is largely defunct, but probably has many of the primitives present that migrated into ActivityPub

Text posts

- [Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org/)
- [Misskey](https://misskey-hub.net/en/)
- [Pleroma](https://pleroma.social/)

Photo posts

- [Pixelfed](https://pixelfed.org/)

Media pins

- [Pinetta](https://codeberg.org/pinetta/pinetta)

Video

- [PeerTube](https://joinpeertube.org/)
- [Owncast](https://owncast.online/) (video streaming)
- [Loops](https://loops.video)

Forums

- [NodeBB](https://nodebb.org/)
- [Lemmy](https://join-lemmy.org/)
- [Kbin](https://kbin.pub/)
- [Mbin](https://github.com/MbinOrg/mbin)
- [Piefed](https://join.piefed.social/)
- [Lotide](https://git.sr.ht/~vpzom/lotide) (link aggregator)
- [Brutalinks](https://git.sr.ht/~mariusor/brutalinks) (link aggregator)

Blogs

- [WriteFreely](https://writefreely.org/)
- [Bookwyrm](https://joinbookwyrm.com/) (book reviews)
- [Plume](https://joinplu.me/)

Micro-blogs

- [Friendica](http://friendi.ca/)
- [Hubzilla](https://hubzilla.org/)
- [Castopod](https://castopod.org/) (podcasts)
- [wafrn](https://app.wafrn.net/dashboard/exploreLocal)

Audio

- [Funkwhale](https://funkwhale.audio/)

Events

- [Mobilizion](https://mobilizon.org/)
- [Gancio](https://gancio.org/)

Classified ads

- [Flohmarkt](https://codeberg.org/flohmarkt/flohmarkt)

Social bookmarking

- [Postmarks](https://postmarks.glitch.me/)

3D print sharing

- [Manyfold](https://manyfold.app/)

There are also ActivityPub plugins for WordPress, Drupal, xwiki, and others.

- Even [Nextcloud](https://nextcloud.com/) has an ActivityPub integration.
- [Ghost](https://fedidb.org/software/ghost) is a CMS based on ActivityPub
