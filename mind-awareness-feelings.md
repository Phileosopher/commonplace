
# How to develop awareness of feelings

It is entirely possible to develop a strong [self-awareness](mind-awareness.md) over thoughts, but without awareness over our feelings.

- This becomes more common in proportion to our [intelligence](people-personality-ocean.md), since every new thought often carries new feelings with it.

Self-discovery is not easy because the anxiety and fear itself actually creates a type of chaos.

- In a sense, the imagery is that it's a hole with snakes coming out of it.

## Find the origin

We must self-examine, but look backward onto the flow of specific [feelings](mind-feelings.md) we possess.

- It's not uncommon to develop a cascade of feelings that culminate into a much later [action](results.md).
- Typically, that flow of feelings was programmed into us by our [parent](parenting-children.md) (more often our mother at first), and it rarely needs modification until we create an unfavorable consequence.
- Since we usually have generated many mental [habits](habits.md) by the time we detect a need to change, we've usually combined all the feelings together into a mixed experience, and have to parse it out.

Frequently, we maintain many beliefs about the world that we presume are factual, but are in fact feelings.

- Further, we often think our feelings *are* us, but are in fact simply an expression of something we are experiencing.

## Complexities

Most of the time, difficulty processing feelings means there are at least two of them in the same space or over the same idea.

[Anger](mind-feelings-anger.md), in particular, is a secondary emotion, so detecting it means we have more to examine.

- The illusion of anger comes in how we tend to unveil anger *after* we've gotten past some other emotion that suppresses it (e.g, [shame](mind-feelings-shame.md)).

Further, over time, our feelings develop more complexities that become even more difficult to untangle.

- Anxiety is a fearful state, which then generates [future predictions](mind-imagination.md) we have reason to believe could become true.
- Bitterness is a complex state of prolonged anger, which essentially creates a constant [conflict](people-conflicts.md) with everything.
- [Depression](hardship-depression.md) derives from sadness and hopelessness, which will utterly remove all [meaning](meaning.md) from anything, even things that would otherwise be meaningful.
- [Shame](mind-feelings-shame.md) is a self-inflicted [image](people-image.md) that sabotages any peace.

Thankfully, there are easy fixes to break us from complex emotions.

- The cure for all anxiety is to focus on [death](hardship-death.md).
- The cure for all bitterness is to remove all sense of [responsibility](meaning.md).
- The cure for all depression is to find [purposes](purpose.md) that create [results](results.md) interpreted as favorable.
- The cure for all shame is to find a sense of self-worth independent of actions that can be performed.

## Making progress

The number one way to know you're making progress in emotional self-awareness is when you aren't reacting to your feelings.

- This non-reaction includes *productive* activities as well, since impulsive and healthy behaviors (like [cleaning](home-housekeeping.md) or writing things down) are still just as mentally oblivious.
- Most of this comes from shifting our [identity](people-identity.md) away from us "being" our feelings and toward us "being" a soul that experiences those feelings.
