
# Various forms of common grace God has given humanity

God obviusly *can* do absolutely anything, but He has placed *many* constraints on Himself for the sake of [love](people-love.md) toward us.

This is a list of ways that God is broadly graceful.

- People often aren't creative enough to realize the *many* ways that God would have every right to be more severe.
- To that end, we frequently misinterpret what is happening around us, as if God is delivering His full and readily-delivered wrath on us.

## The Fall

When mankind sinned, God could have destroyed us entirely and immediately.

- He has our design, and could easily have rebuilt us and tried again.

Further, He could have us drop dead *every* time we sin.

He could have let us stay in the Garden and let us eat from the tree of life.

He could have kept His intent for a Messiah to Himself.

## Old Testament

He could have wiped out all humanity with a flood *including* Noah.

He didn't have to use a rainbow as a sign for us.

- for that matter, He didn't need to establish [covenants](god-contracts.md) with us and could simply have directed us.

He could have made the Hebrew law apply to thoughts as much as actions.

He could have killed Moses for his faithlessness, multiple times.

He could have ditched the Hebrews at Sinai when they worshiped Ba'al.

He could have completely destroyed them when they first showed up in Israel.

Every single time the Hebrews veered from Him, He could have given up entirely.

- Instead, He sent a long string of judges.

He could have destroyed Samson entirely for violating his vows.

He didn't need to give Israel a king.

He didn't need to redeem Israel from Goliath through David.

He didn't need to guide Israel through their exile with the prophets.

He didn't need to save a remnant in Israel.

He didnpt need to preserve the Old Testament.

## New Testament

Jesus didn't need to be born.

Jesus didn't need to heal anyone.

Jesus didn't need to die.

Jesus could have not reappeared after dying.

Jesus didn't have to send the Holy Spirit to help us.

## After the New Testament

He didn't have to keep the New Testament canon around or guide believers to consensus.

He didn't have to protect the Church through [endless persecution](hardship-persecution-church.md).

He didn't need to put up with the ever-increasing evil of this age.

He doesn't need to come back.

## Broad realities

He permits evil people to reign and promises to protect His elect.

He gives guidance to people, even when they don't always follow it.
