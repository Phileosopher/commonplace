
# Who the Father is

As part of [the Godhead](theology-god.md), Jesus shares attributes with God, but there are some distinctive qualities regarding Himself specifically.

The Father created everything, and doesn't change whatsoever.

- We know the Father through the Son.
- The Bible has *plenty* to say about the Father, and it could be argued that every single story in the Bible is about Him.

However, at the same time, we are intentionally obstructed to know the Father only through [the Son](theology-god-jesus.md) (Matthew 11:27).
