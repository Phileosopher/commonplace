
# How discrimination works

While some people like to [imagine](mind-imagination.md) that [bias](mind-bias.md) is impossible, that simply isn't the case. For the sake of [purpose](purpose.md), all human brains that aren't low-functioning [autism](mind-neurodivergence-autism.md) generate bias.

Without exception, all people bias themselves toward or against people based on at least a few of the following criteria:

- [Age](maturity.md)
- [Sex](people-gender.md)
- Sexual [identity](people-identity.md)
- Ethnic [culture](people-culture.md)
- Hobby culture
- [Criminal history](legal-crimes.md)
- [Driving](autos-driving.md) history
- [Family](people-family.md) associations
- [Work](jobs-1_why.md) history
- Job status
- Skin color or race
- [Social class](classes.md)
- [Physical attractiveness](people-gender.md)
- Distinguishing features like clothing and [hygiene](people-image.md)

There are various reasons for this:

- Career decisions
- Social status
- Expectations
- We [imagine](mind-imagination.md) what others may think
- [Past pain](hardship-ptsd.md) that may not have present implications
- What we perceive others may be able to [do](results.md), which is often defined by their [aptitude](humanity.md), [status](groups-member.md) in specific [groups](groups-small.md), or our [understanding](mind-understanding.md) of their [maturity](maturity.md)

APPLICATION: While discrimination usually implies shame, we can discriminate more favorably toward things than others may deserve. The clearest indication of this is when we judge [creations](creations.md) by [who their creator is](identity.md) instead of the [quality](values-quality.md) of the piece itself.

Unless we [analyze](logic.md) what people say and ignore their reputation, we're *always* [unfairly](morality-justice.md) biased. However, it's far more expedient than examining the truth of a matter.

Even when we try to remove bias, we often discriminate on [ideas](values.md) alone as theories without any consideration for [history](people-trends.md).

A stereotype is [habitual](habits.md) and [unfair](morality-justice.md) discrimination. Because we're not conscious of it, we make silly overgeneralizations that lose any sense of [logic](logic.md). People can often address it with [humor](humor.md), but when people have suffered [pain](hardship-ptsd.md) from the stereotype (or [others can benefit from it](politics-leftism.md)), they'll miss the joke.

APPLICATION: When we want to [influence](power-influence.md) others who don't want to [understand](mind-understanding.md), we're wasting our time. It's particularly difficult for us to disengage if we're fighting [past trauma](hardship-ptsd.md).

Stereotypes are inherently amoral because we're not aware of them when we build them (and are therefore naive). And, if we persist with them after we're made aware of their inaccuracy, we are committing a low-level type of [evil](morality-evil.md).
