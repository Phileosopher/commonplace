
# The theology of Mary (Mariology)

As part of the Church, Jesus' mother Mary was a legitimately faithful woman.

- However, the historical development of Mary as having any spiritual significance only developed after [several centuries of cultural development](history-church.md).
- The dominant refutation for this comes through Scripture: there is literally nothing referencing Mary beyond the Gospels throughout the New Testament letters.
- This, however, should not undermine the example she sets forward as what a woman of God looks like, which is a side effect of [the Reformation's developments](theology-solae.md).

Historically, Mary was only officially defined as more significant than anyone else in [the 3rd Ecumenical Council in 431 AD](history-church-4_legal.md).

- However, the Early Church Fathers did talk well of her, so it's not fully without merit.
- Most of the theological significance of Mary's presence has to do with how much you believe [politics](politics-conservativeliberal.md) infiltrated the Church by that time.

There are a few [heresies](people-conflicts-christian-4_false.md) about the Virgin Mary:

- Collyridianism: The Virgin Mary, mother of Jesus, is a goddess.
- Virgin birth denial: Jesus was not born of a virgin.
