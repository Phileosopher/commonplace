
# Forensics summarized

Forensics is the act of piecing together information when not all the information is available.

- It uses plenty of [science](science.md) to [logically](logic.md) gather and infer information that was otherwise not present.

This practice is used most dominantly in [criminal justice systems](legal-crimes.md) and archaeology, but also represents itself within many elements of [cybersecurity](cs-infosec-compliance.md).
