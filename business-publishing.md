
# How to make money publishing content

Making money off content in the [Over-Information Age](information.md) is possible, but not always easy.

## 1: Find a demand

You need to find a niche for things people actually want.

- This is usually easiest when it's closely tied to a [career specialization](jobs-specialization.md) you already have.
- However, you can also stumble across it by trial-and-error while covering things you like.

## 2: Capture the information

There are only several ways to distribute the information:

1. [Write](language-writing.md) the information down.
2. Record the information in audio or video.
3. [Program](cs-langs.md) an interactive experience (e.g, a [game](fun-games-cs.md)).

Writing is, by far, the easiest, but will have the most competition.

Recording content, by its design, has all the aspects of [show business](business-show.md).

Programming is *extremely* time-intensive, but you won't have much competition.

- However, unless you make your code [closed-source](legal-ip-floss.md), you can expect others will clone it very quickly.

## 3: Build the product

The content should present as something that people can acquire.

## 4: Create an offer for people

The offer should indicate what you're selling and why people should act on it.

While people still use the conventional e-book or digital download model, it's guaranteed to be [pirated](cs-distsys-p2p-torrent.md) as soon as you gain *any* popularity.

- This model is largely obsolete, and many approaches work *much* better, especially long-term.

The most involved solution is to use the [Value4Value model](marketing-v4v.md).

However, the most ubiquitous model involves a hybrid of free and paid products:

1. Make a steady stream of free content for a long time.
2. Keep building up a devoted fanbase over that time who continue to consume your free content.
3. When the fanbase is large enough, sell paid-for or branded products (e.g., T-shirts, coffee mugs, stickers).

However, never put the formerly free offering behind a paywall or the fans will resent you for it.

## 5: Keep doing it

Consistency and responsiveness are critical, but also not guaranteed.

- There's a perfectly reasonable chance you will *not* make it.

The only way to keep doing it is because you find [meaning](meaning.md) in doing it, even when some days you don't feel like showing up.
