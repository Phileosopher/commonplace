
# Apologetics against interfaith thinking

Often, many people like to pursue a "middle road" in the religion debate by asserting that many faiths can coexist.

- This simply doesn't work logically, since there is no way to reconcile all the differences.
- However, for the sake of coexistence, they assert the importance that all religions are equal, even if all religions do *not* have the same theological sufficiency.
- In practice, most interfaith advocates are actually militant agnostics or [pantheists](spiritual-exercises-evangelism-apologetics-pantheism.md), but haven't fully recognized it yet.

Typically, most people ground their theology on a moral justification, but in practice they're antagonistic to [extremist religions](people-culture-cults.md) that incite violence and unaware of the distinction.
