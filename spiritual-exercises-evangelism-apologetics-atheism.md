## Old vs. New Earth creationism

The scientific community at large advocates [a specific cosmology](https://gainedin.site/myth-secular) derived from the extrapolations of *thousands* of scientific papers:

1. The universe was formed from a Big Bang explosion, though we don't know why and it might involve a multiverse or something else.
2. At some point after trillions of years, life emerged on Earth. It was a single-celled protozoa that appeared from something odd like lightning striking water to create perfect conditions for life.
3. After billions of years, complex life evolved into many of the forms we see today.
4. Some millions of years ago, humanity evolved from apes.

Therefore, any repudiation of any of those domains essentially frames the necessity for intelligent design.

- It's worth noting, however, that the refuge of most atheists in the absence of sufficient evidence is to believe in a "semi-intelligent" designer (i.e., some other imperfect being that simply had advanced technology or some unnamed power).

## Discrediting evolution's timetable

The evolutionary timetable has infiltrated most of society at least somewhat.

- To this end, most cosmological justifications around [the Bible](bible-1_torah-gen.md) tend to give either [Old Earth or New Earth creationism](people-conflicts-christian-2_debatable.md).

There are some inherent flaws in the evolutionary model.

- Charles Darwin himself indicated that the only way his idea would work would be if we found vast amounts of missing links everywhere, which we haven't found.
- Most of the dating is based on carbon-14's radioactive decay, which is an extremely volatile measurement when we don't even know what the carbon density of the original lifeform would have been.
- The geological proof with rock strata has organisms like trees and skeletons that seem to have been preserved across multiple geological eras.
- The taxonomy does accurately go back to the family (e.g., wolves and dogs are all related by a common ancestor), but there are severe gaps in lifeforms to explain [most orders and all classes](science-life-biochem.md).
- The record shows that there were clearly larger animals at one point, but for some reason we don't know they all died off.

The best counter to the evolution assertion is the canopy theory, which validates how a worldwide flood would have happened.

- The idea was that there was a gigantic water vapor layer covering the earth, which empowered several things:
  - The increased air density allowed larger lifeforms like what we've seen.
  - That amount of water would have been sufficient to have created a worldwide flood.
- The increased plant life would have also heavily fertilized the soil.
- We know dinosaurs are genetically the ancestors of modern reptiles, and we also know that reptiles don't stop growing until they reach a constraint in their environment.
- The canopy theory's dense air could also have empowered people to live longer like in Genesis 5.
  - Any "neanderthals" we've found (very few) would have been people who lived hundreds of years, since our foreheads and ears never actually stop growing.

There are other qualities, however, that represent very difficult justifications for evolution to explain.

- For some reason, most higher-order animals love to play (e.g., dolphins, cats). While younglings at play can be training, adults will often maintain play even when there is no more training benefit and when it actually presents a survival risk.
- For an unexplained reason, animals all love when being petted or rubbed. This doesn't make any logical sense, especially with lower-order animals like reptiles and bugs.
- If the universe was created for our enjoyment and experience, this is an easy explanation, but not when everything is configured for survival of the fittest.
